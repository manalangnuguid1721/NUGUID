Beautiful Spots in the Philippines

Explore five unforgettable places across the islands.

Palawan Boracay Banaue Bohol Mayon Volcano

01. El Nido and Coron, Palawan

Palawan is famous for its limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, and clear water. El Nido is popular for island-hopping, snorkeling, and exploring hidden coves.

Learn more about Palawan →

02. Boracay

Boracay’s White Beach is loved for its soft sand and calm blue water. Visitors can swim, enjoy seaside restaurants, and watch colorful sunsets.

Learn more about Boracay →

03. Banaue Rice Terraces, Ifugao

These impressive terraces were carved into the mountains by generations of farmers. They offer beautiful highland views and reflect the region’s rich cultural heritage.

Learn more about the Rice Terraces →

04. Chocolate Hills, Bohol

Bohol’s Chocolate Hills are a landscape of hundreds of rounded hills. During the dry season, their grass turns brown, making them look like giant chocolate mounds.

Learn more about Bohol →

05. Mayon Volcano, Albay

Mayon Volcano is famous for its near-perfect cone shape. Visitors can enjoy views of the volcano and explore the surrounding sights of Albay.

Learn more about Albay →
